Reading habits

How do I find time to read?

Read for 15 minutes at the same time each day, such as before bed or on the bus. A short daily habit beats a long session once a month.

What if I do not like a book?

Stop. Give a book about 50 pages, and if it still does not work for you, pick another one. Reading should not feel like a chore.

How do I remember what I read?

Write one or two sentences about each book when you finish it. This page works the same way: one short line per book.
